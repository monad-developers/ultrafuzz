`boundary-recipes.md` is the human-readable index. `boundary-recipes.json` must set `schema_version` to `"ultrafuzz.boundary-recipes.v1"` and contain `recipes`, `deferred_or_spec_gated`, and `coverage_priorities`. Every recipe includes `id`, `title`, `path`, `workflow`, `public_support`, `setup`, `action_sequence`, `oracle`, `boundary_values`, `expected_classification_if_red`, and `preferred_downstream_lane`; only `finding_ids`, `property_ids`, and `summary` are optional. Use empty arrays for an empty result.
